# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are recruiters and startup founders evaluating Bipul for hiring, collaboration, or product-building opportunities. They are usually scanning quickly, comparing him against many other candidates, and looking for strong signals of trust, clarity, craft, and taste. The site should help them understand who he is, what he has built, how he thinks, and why he would be valuable to work with.

## Product Purpose

This product is Bipul's personal portfolio website. Its purpose is to present his work, thinking, experience, and identity in a way that feels memorable, credible, and distinctly his. Success means the site creates enough trust and curiosity that recruiters and startup founders want to contact him, discuss opportunities, or start a collaboration.

## Brand Personality

The brand should feel user-centric, simple in thought, and excellent in execution. It should communicate trust and creativity at the same time, with the confidence of an established brand but without imitating enterprise polish or generic portfolio trends. The voice should feel calm, thoughtful, capable, and personal, with a clear sense of identity.

## Anti-references

This brand must not feel template-like, overly loud, or generic. It should avoid repetitive portfolio patterns, safe design clichés, and visual decisions that make it feel mass-produced. It should also avoid trying to mimic Coca-Cola directly, the value there is confidence, not brand aesthetics or category cues.

## Design Principles

1. Show identity, not just competence: the site should feel recognizably Bipul's, not like a polished but interchangeable developer template.
2. Build trust through clarity: every section should reduce doubt, make the work easier to understand, and help fast evaluators find what matters quickly.
3. Keep the thinking simple, make the execution excellent: interactions, copy, and layouts should feel easy to follow, while details reveal depth and care.
4. Creativity should support credibility: distinctive visual choices are important, but they should strengthen confidence rather than distract from the work.
5. Make collaboration feel natural: the experience should guide recruiters and founders toward contact, conversation, and next steps without pressure or noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Default to WCAG AA as the baseline. Maintain strong contrast, readable typography, and clear interactive states throughout. Support reduced motion for users who prefer calmer experiences, and avoid relying on color alone to communicate meaning or status.
